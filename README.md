## 💼 What I Do

### **Circuit Design**
I design robust, efficient circuits that meet stringent performance requirements. From analog amplification stages to complex digital interfaces, I ensure reliability and manufacturability at every step.

### **PCB Layout**
Using Cadence Allegro and Altium Designer, I create multi-layer PCBs with optimal routing, proper grounding strategies, and signal integrity considerations. Every design is DRC-clean and manufacturing-ready.

### **Signal Integrity**
I apply HyperLynx for signal integrity analysis, ensuring high-speed designs meet timing requirements and maintain signal quality across the entire product.

### **Hardware Validation**
I conduct comprehensive testing and validation, from prototype troubleshooting to production validation, ensuring products meet specifications and customer expectations.

### **Cost Optimization**
I optimize BOMs and manufacturing processes to reduce costs by 15%+ while maintaining quality and reliability standards.
